Public Education Information Management System (student_data)

student_data is the statewide data collection system used by the Texas Education Agency (TEA) to collect information from Texas public school districts and charter schools.

The system collects:

• Student demographics
• Student academic performance
• Attendance records
• Personnel data
• Financial data
• Organizational data

student_data data is used for accountability ratings, state funding calculations, policy analysis, and statewide education reporting.

All Local Education Agencies (LEAs) are required to submit student_data data to TEA.
